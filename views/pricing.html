Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before choosing a support plan.

How long does a plan last?

The plans displayed on this page are based on a 30-day period.

How do I request assistance?

Submit a support request through the website or use your customer portal where applicable.

Can I get help with business devices?

Business Growth and Corporate SLA display device support limits. Contact IT Elements to discuss your requirements.

What happens when my plan expires?

Plans are for 30 days. Contact IT Elements about renewal before your current plan period ends.

Need Help Choosing a Plan?

If you are unsure which support option is right for you, contact IT Elements and explain what kind of assistance you need.
