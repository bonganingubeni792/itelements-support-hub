COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is remote IT support?

Remote support allows a technician to assist with certain computer problems using an approved remote-access application and your permission.

Can I request onsite support?

Yes. You can submit an onsite support enquiry. Location, availability and any travel charges should be confirmed before the appointment.

How do I track my support request?

Use the IT Elements customer portal to log in and view your support requests and their progress.

Can my business get monthly IT support?

Yes. Explore our business support page and contact us to discuss the available monthly plans and your business requirements.

How do I pay for a service?

Payment options depend on the service or plan. Follow the checkout instructions presented for your selected service.
