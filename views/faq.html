Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about IT Elements support, service plans, payments, remote assistance and your customer portal. If you need more help, our team is available through the contact page.

How can we help?

Here are some useful answers to help you get started.

When is support available?

Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 South African time (SAST). Send a support request and the team can follow up during support hours.

How do I request help?

Use the Request Help form to describe the problem. You can also sign in to the client portal to submit and track tickets.

Do I need a support plan?

No. You can choose a 30-day plan or request one-time hourly incident support. Plan details and prices are listed on the Plans page.

How does payment work?

Pay securely through PayFast hosted checkout. Plans are one-time 30-day payments and require manual renewal. Your plan is activated after PayFast confirms the payment.

When should I provide my remote session password?

Only when you are ready and speaking with an IT Elements engineer. Never put your account password or payment card details in a support ticket.

How can I follow my ticket?

Sign in to your client portal to view support ticket statuses and your plan information.

Keep your information safe

IT Elements will never need you to include your banking password, email password or payment card details in a support request. Share remote-session details only when you are ready to receive assistance.

Still need assistance?

If you cannot find the answer you need, contact us or submit a support request so we can assist you.
