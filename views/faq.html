Frequently asked questions

When is support available?

Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 South African time (SAST). Send a support request and the team can follow up during support hours.

How do I request help?

Use the Request Help form to describe the problem. You can also sign in to the client portal to submit and track tickets.

Do I need a support plan?

No. You can choose a 30-day plan or request one-time hourly incident support. Plan details and prices are listed on the Plans page.

How does payment work?

Payments are completed through Yoco’s hosted checkout. Plans are one-time 30-day payments and require manual renewal.

When should I provide my remote session password?

Only when you are ready and speaking with an IT Elements engineer. Never put your account password or payment card details in a support ticket.

How can I follow my ticket?

Sign in to your client portal to view support ticket statuses and your plan information.
