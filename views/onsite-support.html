The process

How to request support

01

Tell us about the problem

Complete the appointment request with your contact details, selected service and a description of the issue.

02

We review your request

IT Elements will review the information and contact you to discuss the service, availability and pricing.

03

Confirm the appointment

Your appointment is confirmed only after the details and availability have been agreed with IT Elements.

Choose your support type

Remote or on-site support

On-site support

Request assistance at your location. Include your area or address in the appointment request so the details can be discussed before an appointment is confirmed.

Remote support

If your issue may be handled remotely, select remote support in the appointment request. IT Elements can discuss the appropriate next step with you.

Important appointment information

Submitting an appointment request does not confirm a booking. IT Elements will contact you to confirm availability and the final price. Starting prices may change depending on the work required. Do not send passwords or remote-access credentials through the appointment form.
