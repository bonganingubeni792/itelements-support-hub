About IT Elements

Clear, approachable computer support.

IT Elements Support helps people and small organisations in South Africa request remote IT assistance, choose a support plan and follow their support tickets through a customer portal.

Our aim is to make it straightforward to explain a technical problem, understand the available support options and arrange a secure session when remote access is needed.
