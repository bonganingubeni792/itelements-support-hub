HELP CENTRE

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before requesting support.

Do you offer remote IT support?

Yes. You can request remote assistance. The available support method and any required setup will be discussed before work begins.

Can I request onsite assistance?

Yes. Select Onsite Support in the request form. Availability, location and any travel costs will be confirmed with you.

Are the displayed prices final?

Displayed prices are starting prices for selected services. The final cost may depend on the complexity of the problem and any additional work required. We will discuss this with you before proceeding.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. Submitting the form sends a service enquiry. Your appointment is only confirmed after availability and arrangements have been agreed.

Can businesses request IT support?

Yes. Businesses can request assistance with computers, networks, software and general IT requirements. Select Business IT Support to get started.
